Our foundations

Built on four pillars

Everything we build flows from these core values — so recovery is yours, on your terms.

Anonymity

Zero-knowledge proofs and proof-of-humanity technology mean you're real — but never exposed. Your identity stays yours.

Spirituality

Drawing from AA, CBT, complex trauma models, meditation and art therapy — recovery pathways that honour the whole person.

Community

Peer-governed channels, group sessions with avatars, and a DAO where the community steers its own recovery infrastructure.

Self-Sovereignty

On-chain reputation, DeFi incentives, and tokenised milestones give you ownership of your recovery journey — and your economic future.

The journey

How RecoHubz works

From first visit to long-term empowerment — every step is anonymous, voluntary and bespoke to you.

1

Land & Explore

Arrive anonymously. Browse the resource library, read about high-functioning recovery patterns, and learn what RecoHubz offers — no sign-up required at this stage.

2

Prove Humanity, Not Identity

Join using Proof-of-Humanity (ZK) — we confirm you're a real person without ever knowing who you are. Sign in via Gmail or wallet. Accept community guidelines.

3

AI Triage & Questionnaire

An AI guide walks you through a private questionnaire covering your situation, employment history and wellbeing. It maps your needs and suggests a bespoke pathway.

4

Choose Your Path

Select from automated therapy information, real-world therapist referral, community support, or a hybrid pathway — all at your pace, all pseudonymous.

5

Grow & Contribute

Build your on-chain recovery reputation, access the job board, join community channels, and participate in DAO governance — turning recovery into empowerment.

What's inside

Everything in one protocol

RecoHubz combines tools that have never existed together in one private, decentralised space.

AI Triage

Personalised Assessment

AI-driven questionnaire identifies your situation and maps you to relevant therapy types, resources and community channels from day one.

Community

Discord-Like Channels

Topic-specific channels with avatar-based anonymity. Group sessions facilitated by addiction specialists. Peer support that actually feels safe.

Repository

Recovery Resource Library

Curated AA, CA, CBT and complex trauma resources. Meditation, music and art therapy guides. All freely accessible, always available.

Web3

On-Chain Reputation

Track recovery milestones anonymously on-chain. Build verifiable records of contribution and progress that travel with you — not tied to your name.

Economy

Self-Sustaining DeFi

A DAO-governed treasury funded by DeFi yield strategies. Funds flow back into care, incentives and platform development — not to shareholders.

Employment

Job Board & Credit

For those ready for a new start — access job listings, build creditworthiness on-chain, and transition from recovery into economic participation.
